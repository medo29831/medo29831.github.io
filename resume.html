📄 HTML Resume Page

Learn how to create a professional resume using HTML.

📖 Project Overview

In this project, you will build a personal resume webpage. The page includes your personal information, education, skills, work experience, and contact details.

📋 Requirements

  • Basic knowledge of HTML.
  • Understanding of headings and paragraphs.
  • Knowledge of lists.
  • Basic knowledge of links.

💻 Source Code


<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>

<head>
    <title>My Resume</title>
</head>

<body>

<h1>Mohamed</h1>

<h2>About Me</h2>
<p>
I am a beginner web developer learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
</p>

<h2>Education</h2>
<ul>
    <li>High School Student</li>
</ul>

<h2>Skills</h2>
<ul>
    <li>HTML</li>
    <li>CSS</li>
    <li>JavaScript</li>
</ul>

<h2>Contact</h2>
<p>
Email: example@email.com
</p>

</body>

</html>

📖 Code Explanation

1. Add Your Name

Use the <h1> tag to display your name.

2. About Me

Write a short paragraph introducing yourself.

3. Education

List your educational background.

4. Skills

Use an unordered list to display your skills.

5. Contact Information

Provide an email address or other contact details.

🖥️ Output

📄 My Resume

🎯 Exercise

Create your own resume webpage that includes:

  • Your full name.
  • A short "About Me" section.
  • Your education.
  • Your skills.
  • Your contact information.
